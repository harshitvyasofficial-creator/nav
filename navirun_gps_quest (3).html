How to play

1. Start outside in a safe, open area and allow location access.
2. Walk toward the marked destination; listen for voice prompts.
3. Get within about 25 metres to claim 100 points.
4. The next target increases by 100 metres each time and its direction changes randomly.
5. Finish before the 10-minute timer reaches zero. Take turns with a friend and compare scores.

Targets are generated nearby and routed on mapped roads when the routing service responds. Actual route length may differ from the target distance. Do not enter private property, cross unsafe roads, or walk while staring at your phone.
